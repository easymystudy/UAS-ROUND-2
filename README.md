2. `task2`
    In this Folder there is Solution of Task-2 (Bonus Problem Statement)

    `Problem Statement-1`
    If you are able to complete the given tasks within the set time limit , this is a bonus task
    , one more algorithmically complex and advanced where you are given an image with red and
    green regions green traversible and red non traversible , you are the white circle and your goal is
    to reach the black circle , the catch is that you cant see the whole image at once your receptive
    field (the area you can see is limited) it is a (width/4 x height/2) area of the entire image, you need
    to calculate the dimensions yourself , your goal is to make the white circle walk towards the black
    circle , the suggested list of steps to follow is such :
    1) crop out the mask of the white circle
    2) crop the image into the defined receptive field
    3) the circle can move 20 pixels per step you can only move in one of the four directions up ,
    down , left , right . So if you want to go diagonal you need to move two steps .
    4) you cannot pass through the walls and need to reach the black target circle.
    5) save a snapshot after every step until you reach the black target.
    6) finally compile the saved snapshots into a video.
